1. Capture, Import & 14-Layer Live Calibration Pipeline

MakingFaces turns any live camera frame or external image into a calibrated, DMX-ready character layer in three seamless stages:

STAGE 1 • CAPTURE

Camera & USB Microscope (Ctrl+P)

  • Probes /dev/video0..3 (USB V4L2) or Pi CSI (rpicam-still).
  • R rotates feed 90° for vertically mounted webcams.
  • Space/Enter snaps a true 3:4 portrait photo (full head incl. hair & chin) straight to import/.
  • E captures a close-up microscope iris photo pre-assigned to EYE_IRIS.
STAGE 2 • CUTOUT & INPAINT

Calibration Studio (Ctrl+I)

  • Corner-sampled + white flood-fill removes studio backgrounds (W); adjust tolerance live with B / Shift+B.
  • Drag/Wheel fits the image; click any cyan eye/nose/mouth oval (turns bright green) to position cutouts (V) and OpenCV Telea erasure (N).
  • Enter exports transparent PNGs to assets/ & updates packs/default.zip.
STAGE 3 • ON-STAGE FIT

14-Layer Live Calibration (TAB)

  • Press TAB to select any of the 14 layers (EYE_LEFT..HAT, MAKEUP, BG, FX).
  • Arrows move X/Y (Shift = 10 px).
  • Alt+Up/Down scales 50%–160%.
  • Ctrl+Left/Right adjusts symmetrical split distance.
  • S saves per-face profile to makingfaces.ini.
